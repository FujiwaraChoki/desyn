## Plans

- **Free** — a deliberately basic plan to try the editor: the full canvas and core tools, basic templates and PNG, JPEG and PDF export. No AI features.
- **Pro** — everything, for one price, with a monthly pool of AI credits under fair use.
- **Self-hosted** — every feature with no credit pool or limits, on your own server, free for non-commercial use. You bring the AI provider keys or local models. See [self-hosting](/self-hosting).

## Changing plans

Upgrade, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel from **Settings → Billing**. Payments and invoices are handled through Stripe's customer portal.
