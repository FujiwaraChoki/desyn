## Workspaces

A workspace is where designs, folders, brand kits and uploads live. Everyone starts with a personal workspace. On Pro you can create up to five workspaces (unlimited when self-hosted) — for example one per client or team — and switch between them from the logo menu.

Inside a workspace, organize designs in **folders**. Archived designs go to **Trash**, where you can restore them or delete them for good.

## Member roles

Each member of a workspace has exactly one role:

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing, deleting the workspace and transferring ownership to another member. |
| **Admin** | Manage members and invites, brand kits, folders and every design. |
| **Editor** | Create and edit designs, use AI tools, upload assets. |
| **Commenter** | View designs and add or resolve comments. |
| **Viewer** | View designs. |

Invite people from **Settings → Members** — up to 20 addresses at once. They receive an email with a link (`/accept/…`) and join with the role you chose; they must sign in with the invited address. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent or revoked. Admins can change roles or remove members at any time; removing someone immediately ends their access.

> [!NOTE]
> Live collaboration, comments and teams are Pro features. On Pro, each design can have up to 25 collaborators.

## Sharing a single design

You do not need to add someone to the workspace to show them a design. From **Share** in the editor, create a link:

- **View** — anyone with the link can view (available on every plan).
- **Comment** — they can view and comment.
- **Edit** — they can edit (Pro).

Links can have a password and an expiry date, and you can revoke them at any time. Presentations can also be shared as a presenter link at `/present/…`.

## API keys belong to people

[Agent API](/developers/api) keys are created by a member in **Settings → API keys** and act with that member's permissions in the chosen workspace, limited further by the key's scopes.
