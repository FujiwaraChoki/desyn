---
title: Brand kits
description: Store your colors, fonts, logos, tone of voice and rules once, then apply them to any design by hand or with the AI.
---

A brand kit is everything that makes your work recognizably yours, in one place:

- **Colors** — a named palette in order of importance (primary first).
- **Fonts** — which families to use for headings, subheadings, body and accents, including uploaded custom fonts.
- **Logos** — light and dark variants, marks and wordmarks.
- **Voice** — how your copy should sound, in a sentence or two (*"warm, unhurried, never shouty"*).
- **Rules** — hard constraints the AI must respect (*"never put the logo on a photo"*, *"headlines in sentence case"*).
- **Guidelines** — an optional longer document; the AI reads it when it plans designs.

Brand kits are part of Pro (up to 10 per workspace) and unlimited when self-hosted.

## Creating a kit

Open **Brand** on the dashboard (or the **Brand** panel in the editor) and choose **New brand kit**. Fill it in by hand, or let Desyn extract it:

- **From a design** — pick a design you like; Desyn reads its palette, fonts and logo usage.
- **From images or a PDF** — upload a logo, a brand book or a few examples; the AI proposes colors, fonts and a voice that you review before saving.

## Using a kit

- In the editor, the **Brand** panel shows your kit's colors, text styles and logos. Click to apply, or drag a logo onto the page.
- A design can **follow** a kit. Its colors become variables such as `brand.primary`, so switching the kit restyles every layer that uses them.
- Ask the agent: *"make this match my brand"*. It maps colors, fonts, logos and copy tone to the kit and changes only what is off-brand. See [brand consistency](/ai/brand-consistency).
- Workflows such as [campaigns](/ai/workflows#campaign) and template instantiation accept a brand kit, so everything they produce starts on-brand.

> [!TIP]
> Write the voice as instructions, not adjectives alone: *"Short sentences. Speak to one person. No exclamation marks."* gives the AI much more to work with than *"friendly"*.
