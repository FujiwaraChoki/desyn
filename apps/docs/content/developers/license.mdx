## What can I do for free?

Use, copy, modify and run Desyn, and distribute unmodified or modified copies, for **non-commercial use**:

- personal projects and hobby work,
- education and academic research,
- evaluating the software,
- use by charitable, educational or public-interest organizations that are not run for profit.

Self-hosting for these purposes is free, with every feature unlocked.

## What counts as commercial use?

Any use primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation, including:

- offering Desyn or a derivative as a hosted, managed or white-label service,
- selling, sublicensing, renting or bundling it with a paid product,
- using it inside a for-profit organization's business operations — including hosting it for a company's own employees or customers,
- using its output as a core part of a paid service.

## Can my company self-host Desyn for its designers?

Not under the free license: running it inside a for-profit organization's operations is commercial use. You need a commercial license — or use the hosted Pro plan at desyn.app.

## Can I use designs I made with Desyn commercially?

The license governs the **software**. Using the hosted app at desyn.app under its terms of service is separate from licensing the source code. On a self-hosted installation running under the non-commercial license, using its output as a core part of a paid service is commercial use. For anything in between, contact us.

## Can I modify it and publish my changes?

Yes, for non-commercial use. Keep the license, copyright notice and attribution; clearly mark your version as modified; and pass the same license on to anyone you distribute it to. You may not remove or bypass license notices.

## What about the name and logo?

"Desyn", the sun mark and desyn.app are not licensed. Do not use them to imply endorsement or to name a derivative in a way that could be confused with Desyn.

## What happens if I break the terms?

Your license ends automatically. For a first violation, it is reinstated if you fix the problem within 30 days of notice.

## Third-party components and fonts
