## Fills

Fills are painted bottom to top. Every fill can have its own opacity, visibility and blend mode. Gradient and image coordinates are normalized to the node's box (`0..1`).

<Generated name="model-types" names="Fill,Stop,ColorRef" />

- `linear` — `angle` in degrees.
- `radial` — center `cx`, `cy` and radii `rx`, `ry`.
- `conic` — center and start `angle`.
- `image` — `mode` `fill` (cover), `fit` (contain), `tile` or `stretch`, with optional scale, offset, rotation and per-fill adjustments.

Colors are lowercase hex (`#rrggbb` or `#rrggbbaa`). Where noted, a color can be a `ColorRef` instead: `{ "$var": "brand.primary" }` resolves through `Design.styles.variables`, `{ "$style": "<id>" }` through `Design.styles.colors`.

## Strokes

<Generated name="model-types" names="Stroke" />

A stroke's paint is a list of fills, so strokes can be gradients or images. `dash` is a dash/gap pattern in pixels.

## Effects

<Generated name="model-types" names="Effect" />

Effects render in order. `backgroundBlur` blurs what is behind the node within its shape; `noise` adds grain (`mono` for grayscale grain); `glow` is a colored blur around the node's alpha.

## Image adjustments

<Generated name="model-types" names="ImageAdjust" />

Every adjustment is optional in image fills (`Partial<ImageAdjust>`) and complete on image nodes. `duotone` maps shadows and highlights to two colors; `filter` names a preset.

## Animation

<Generated name="model-types" names="Animation" />

`phase` decides when an animation plays: `in` when the page appears, `out` before it ends, `loop` continuously. `startMs` and `durationMs` are relative to the page's start. Page timing (`PageNode.durationMs`) and transitions (`PageNode.transition`) are described in the [nodes reference](/developers/document-model/nodes#containers).
