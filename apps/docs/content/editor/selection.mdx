## Selecting

- Click a layer to select it. Clicking inside a group selects the whole group; double-click (or press <Keys combo="enter" />) to go inside, and <Keys combo="shift+enter" /> to select the parent again.
- <Keys combo="mod+click" /> selects the deepest layer under the pointer directly, ignoring groups.
- <Keys combo="shift+click" /> adds or removes layers from the selection.
- Drag on empty canvas to select everything the marquee touches.
- <Keys combo="mod+a" /> selects everything on the page; <Keys combo="escape" /> deselects.
- <Keys combo="tab" /> and <Keys combo="shift+tab" /> move the selection to the next or previous sibling — a quick way to step through layers with the keyboard.

Locked layers cannot be selected on the canvas; select them in the [Layers panel](/editor/layers-and-pages) instead.

## Moving

Drag the selection, or nudge it with the arrow keys: one pixel per press, ten with <Keys combo="shift" />. Hold <Keys combo="alt" /> while dragging to duplicate as you move. Layers snap to guides, the grid and their neighbors as described in [canvas & navigation](/editor/canvas#snapping).

Drag a layer into a frame to put it inside; drag it out to take it out. In an [auto-layout](/editor/auto-layout) frame, dragging reorders children instead of placing them freely.

## Resizing and rotating

Drag a handle to resize. Hold <Keys combo="shift" /> to keep proportions (or turn on the aspect lock in the **Position** tab), and <Keys combo="alt" /> to resize from the center. Resizing a text layer changes its box, not its font size.

Move the pointer just outside a corner handle to rotate. Hold <Keys combo="shift" /> to snap to 15° steps. Flip horizontally with <Keys combo="shift+h" /> and vertically with <Keys combo="shift+v" />.

## Exact values

The **Position** tab of the inspector shows X, Y, width, height and rotation. Every number field accepts arithmetic (`120*2`, `+16`, `50%`) and supports scrubbing: drag the label left or right to change the value, with <Keys combo="shift" /> for bigger steps. When several layers are selected, editing a field sets it on all of them; fields that differ show *Mixed*.

## Align and distribute

With two or more layers selected, use the **Arrange** tab or the shortcuts:

- Align left, center or right: <Keys combo="alt+a" />, <Keys combo="alt+h" />, <Keys combo="alt+d" />
- Align top, middle or bottom: <Keys combo="alt+w" />, <Keys combo="alt+v" />, <Keys combo="alt+s" />
- Distribute horizontally or vertically: <Keys combo="alt+shift+h" />, <Keys combo="alt+shift+v" />

With a single layer selected, alignment is relative to its parent frame or page. **Tidy up** arranges a messy selection into an even row, column or grid, and **Match size** makes every selected layer the size of the first.

## Order

Bring forward and send backward with <Keys combo="mod+]" /> and <Keys combo="mod+[" />; add <Keys combo="alt" /> to go all the way to the front or back.

## Group, frame and mask

- **Group** (<Keys combo="mod+g" />) bundles layers without adding any visual. **Ungroup** with <Keys combo="mod+shift+g" />.
- **Frame selection** (<Keys combo="mod+alt+g" />) wraps the layers in a frame you can style, clip and give auto-layout.
- **Use as mask** (<Keys combo="mod+alt+m" />) turns the bottom selected layer into a mask for the layers above it in the same container: they are only visible where the mask is opaque.

## Constraints

When a frame resizes, its children follow their constraints: pinned to the left, right or center, stretched with the frame, or scaled proportionally — set separately for horizontal and vertical. Constraints are what make [resizing a design](/ai/workflows#resize) to other formats look intentional.
