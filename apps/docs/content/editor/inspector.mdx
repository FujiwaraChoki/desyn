title: The inspector

The inspector on the right edits whatever is selected. With nothing selected, it shows the current page: size, unit, background, bleed, layout grid and notes. Its tabs are always in the same place so your hands learn where things are.

## Design

The look of the selection:

- **Fills** and **strokes** — see [fills, strokes & effects](/editor/effects).
- **Text** — font, size, weight, spacing, alignment, lists, case and decoration for text layers. See [text](/editor/text).
- **Image** — fit, crop, shape, adjustments, filters and alt text for images. See [images](/editor/images).
- **Shape** — corner radius and smoothing, sides, points, arcs.
- **Chart, table, QR** — the data and appearance of those elements. See [charts, tables & QR](/editor/charts-tables-qr).
- **Opacity** and **blend mode**.
- **Role** — the [semantic role](/concepts/roles) of the layer.

## Arrange

How layers relate to each other: align and distribute, tidy up into rows or grids, match sizes, layer order, and the [auto-layout](/editor/auto-layout) settings of a frame.

## Effects

Shadows, blurs, noise and glow, plus effect styles. See [effects](/editor/effects#effects).

## Animate

Entrance, exit and looping animations for presentations and video, with timing and easing, and page transitions. See [animation & video](/editor/animation-and-video).

## Position

Exact geometry: X and Y (relative to the parent), width and height with the aspect lock, rotation, flips, constraints, auto-layout sizing (fixed, hug, fill), and links (to a URL or another page).

## Editing values

Every number field in the inspector works the same way:

- Type a value, or arithmetic such as `120/3` or `+8`.
- Scrub: drag the field's label left or right; hold <Keys combo="shift" /> for larger steps.
- Use <Keys combo="arrowup" /> and <Keys combo="arrowdown" /> in a focused field to step by 1, or by 10 with <Keys combo="shift" />.
- With several layers selected, fields that differ show *Mixed*; setting a value sets it on all of them.

Colors accept hex (`#ffb02e`), and the swatch opens the full color picker. Linked values — a color style or a brand variable — show the style's name; detach to edit the value directly.

## Mixed selections

When you select different kinds of layers, the inspector shows only what they have in common. Selecting text inside a text layer narrows the controls to character and paragraph styles for just that range.
