Desyn has a small vocabulary. Once these ideas click, the editor, the AI and the API all behave the way you expect, because they share one model underneath.

## The short version

- A **design** is one document: a poster, a deck, a video. It has a kind, a name and one or more **pages**.
- A page holds **layers** (also called nodes): frames, groups, shapes, text, images, SVGs, videos, charts, tables and QR codes, stacked back to front.
- Layers can carry a **semantic role** — headline, photo, price, CTA — that tells the AI what they are for.
- Designs live in a **workspace**, where members have a role (owner, admin, editor, commenter, viewer).
- A **brand kit** stores colors, fonts, logos, voice and rules so the AI and you can apply them anywhere.
- A **template** is a ready-made design with named **slots** you fill in.
- **Credits** measure AI usage on the hosted Pro plan. Your own keys and local models do not use them.

Every change to a design — by you, a collaborator, the agent or a workflow — is a **transaction**: a labeled list of operations that applies atomically, syncs to everyone in real time, and undoes as one step.
