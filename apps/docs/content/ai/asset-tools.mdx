Asset tools create or transform images and vectors. Use them from the **AI** panel, from the context toolbar on a selected image, or by asking the agent. Every result is saved to your library as a new asset — the original is never overwritten — with the provider, model and prompt recorded so you can regenerate or trace where it came from.

## Generate image

Describe the image and choose a size or aspect ratio. Desyn picks the best available image model for the job, including a model that renders legible text inside images when your prompt asks for lettering.

```text
Ceramic coffee cup on warm linen, morning sun from the left, soft shadows,
shallow depth of field, editorial product photography
```

If the design follows a [brand kit](/concepts/brand-kits), its palette and mood are taken into account. Generated images can be placed directly into a selected frame or image layer.

## Generate SVG

Creates vector illustrations, icons and patterns as editable SVG: *"a minimal line icon of a kiln"*, *"an abstract wave pattern in the brand colors"*. SVGs are sanitized before they are stored, and their colors can be remapped in the inspector afterwards.

## Remove background

Separates the subject from its background. The result is stored as a mask on the image, so the original pixels are kept and you can switch the mask off later. Depending on configuration it runs on the server with a dedicated segmentation model or directly in your browser.

## Inpaint

Paint over part of an image and describe what should be there instead — remove a stray object, change a color, add a detail. Everything outside the painted area stays the same.

## Outpaint

Extends an image beyond its edges: make a square photo fit a wide banner, or add room above a subject for a headline. Choose the new size; the model fills the new area to match.

## Upscale

Increases resolution by 2× or 4× while adding plausible detail — useful for print and for small images flagged as low resolution.

## Mockups

Places your design or an image into a realistic scene: a poster on a wall, a phone screen, a book cover, a mug or a business card on a desk. Pick a scene and a page; the mockup is generated as a new image you can use anywhere.

## Trace to vector

Converts a bitmap — a logo, a sketch, simple artwork — into vector paths with fills, so it scales cleanly and can be recolored and edited with the [pen tool](/editor/shapes-and-pen).

## Video clips

On installations with a video-capable provider, the agent can generate short video clips for video designs. This is the most expensive tool; check the cost below before using it at scale.
