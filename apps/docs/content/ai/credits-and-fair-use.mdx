The figures on this page come straight from `config/plans.json`, the file the product itself reads.

## The monthly pool

Pro includes **600 credits per month**. Credits are a simple measure of AI work: an agent request, a generated image, a resized variant. The pool refills at the start of each period. The Free plan does not include AI; self-hosted installations have no credit pool at all.

See your balance, recent usage and history in **Settings → Usage**, or with the API at [`GET /credits`](/developers/api/account#get-credit-balance).

## What things cost

<Generated name="credit-costs" />

A few examples:

- Asking the agent to restyle a slide: **1 credit** (one agent turn), however many layers it changes.
- Generating a full poster that needs two images: **5 + 2 × 3 = 11 credits**.
- Resizing one design into six formats: **6 credits**.
- Localizing a design into four languages: **4 credits**.

Credits are reserved when a run starts and settled when it finishes, so a cancelled or failed run returns whatever it did not use.

## When you run out

There are **no overage charges** — you are never billed beyond your plan. When the monthly pool is used up, AI requests keep working under a **soft throttle**: each request waits a little before it starts (between 1.5 and 6 seconds). Your pool refills at the start of the next period.

## Rate limits

These limits keep the service fast for everyone. Hitting a per-minute or per-day cap returns a clear message with when you can continue; the [API](/developers/api#rate-limits) returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header.

## Bring your own key

Requests that run on **your own provider key** (BYOK) or on a **local model** never spend credits. Add a key for OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, Groq, Fal or Replicate in **Settings → AI providers** and choose it for any task. See [providers & BYOK](/ai/providers-and-byok).

## Fair use

Pro is priced for one person's real design work. Patterns that look like abuse — bulk account creation, automated scraping, or reselling access — are detected and can lead to throttling or suspension. If your work genuinely needs more, bring your own key or [self-host](/self-hosting).
