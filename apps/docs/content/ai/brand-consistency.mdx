Brand kits ([concepts](/concepts/brand-kits)) hold your colors, fonts, logos, voice and rules. The AI uses them in three ways.

## "Make this match my brand"

Select a design that follows a kit — or tell the agent which kit to use — and ask:

```text
Make this match my brand.
```

The agent runs `apply_brand`, which maps the design onto the kit rather than repainting it:

- **Colors** — each color in the design is matched to the closest brand color by role and contrast, keeping text readable. Brand colors become variables (such as `brand.primary`) so later kit changes flow through.
- **Fonts** — headline-like text gets the heading font, body text the body font, using [roles](/concepts/roles) where they exist and visual hierarchy where they do not.
- **Logos** — placeholder or outdated logos are replaced with the right variant for the background (light or dark).
- **Voice** — copy is lightly rewritten in your tone, keeping facts, names and numbers intact. Say *"don't change the copy"* to skip this.
- **Rules** — hard rules from the kit are checked and fixed (*"logo never on photos"*, *"minimum 24 px headlines"*).

You can scope it: *"apply the brand to the selected card only"*, or *"only fix the colors"*.

## On-brand from the start

When a design follows a kit, everything the AI creates uses it: generated designs, new pages, [workflows](/ai/workflows), template remixes and generated images (whose prompts include the palette and mood). The agent prefers your saved text and color styles over new values.

## Checking a design

Ask *"is this on brand?"* and the agent compares the design with the kit and lists what is off — a color that is not in the palette, an extra font, a logo with too little clear space — before fixing anything you approve.

## Extracting a brand kit

Starting from scratch? Create a kit from existing material:

- **From a design** you like — the agent reads its palette, fonts and logo usage.
- **From files** — upload your logo, a brand guidelines PDF or a few example images; the agent proposes colors, fonts, a voice description and rules.

Review the proposal, adjust anything, and save. Brand kits are part of Pro (up to 10 per workspace) and the self-hosted edition.
