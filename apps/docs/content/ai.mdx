Desyn's AI is built into the document, not bolted on. It works through the same operations as the editor, which has three consequences you will notice right away:

1. **Everything it makes is editable.** Generated designs are frames, text, shapes and images — never a flat picture of a design.
2. **It only changes what you ask for.** Untouched layers stay exactly as they were, down to their ids, so comments, links and your own tweaks survive.
3. **Every request is one undo step**, visible live to collaborators and recorded in version history with an "AI:" label.

## Where to find it

- The **AI dock**: a prompt bar at the bottom center of the editor (<Keys combo="mod+j" />) that expands into a chat with a run log.
- The **AI panel** in the left rail: one-click asset tools and workflows.
- **Context actions**: right-click a selection or use the floating toolbar for actions like *Remove background*, *Rewrite* or *Translate*.
- **Templates**: *Remix with AI* adapts a template to your brief.
- **Outside the editor**: the [Agent API and MCP server](/developers/api) give your own agents the same tools.

## Availability

AI features are part of **Pro** (with a monthly credit pool, see [credits & fair use](/ai/credits-and-fair-use)) and the **self-hosted** edition (with your own provider keys or local models). The Free plan does not include AI features.
