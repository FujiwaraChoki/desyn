Desyn is a complete design tool in the spirit of Canva and Figma: posters, social posts, presentations, documents, videos, websites and more, on an infinite canvas with pages, auto-layout, a pen tool, effects and real-time collaboration. What makes it different is that the AI is not a sidebar that pastes pictures. It reads your design the way a designer does — structure, typography, spacing, hierarchy, what you have selected — and edits it through the same operations you use, so every change is a normal, undoable step made of real layers.

## What you can make

Every design has a kind, which sets sensible sizes, margins and the AI's sense of what good looks like: social posts and stories, posters and flyers, thumbnails, banners and ads, presentations, infographics, business cards, resumes, invitations, menus, logos, newsletters, documents, whiteboards, one-page websites, videos, book covers, certificates, stickers and mockups — or a custom size.

Start from a blank page, from one of the templates (every template is a real, editable design with named slots), or from a sentence: *"a launch poster for a ceramic coffee cup, warm and minimal"*.

## How the AI fits in

The agent can do four kinds of work, and you can mix them in one conversation:

- **Generate** complete designs as editable layers: it plans the layout, writes the copy, picks fonts and colors, generates the images it needs, builds the page, looks at the result and fixes what it does not like.
- **Edit** conversationally: *"make the headline shorter and warmer"*, *"turn this into a three-column layout"*, *"translate every page to German"*. Elements you did not mention stay exactly as they were.
- **Understand** the canvas: ask *"why does this feel cluttered?"* and it answers from a structural description of the page, including contrast and spacing problems it can fix.
- **Create assets**: images, SVG illustrations, background removal, inpainting, outpainting, upscaling, mockups and tracing bitmaps into vectors.
